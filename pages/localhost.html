2026 Local Co-Host

India Internet Research Organisation (IIRO)

IIRO is a volunteer-driven research and policy think tank dedicated to studying and shaping India's Internet ecosystem. Operating under the motto "Research, Innovate, and Empower Technology", IIRO brings together students, researchers, and policymakers to drive meaningful change in the digital world.

Its work spans Internet Governance, DNS policy, cybersecurity, digital rights, and emerging technologies. Through workshops, publications, and collaborative initiatives, IIRO empowers India's next generation of digital leaders to engage with global Internet policy processes.

O.P. Jindal Global University (JGU)

O.P. Jindal Global University (JGU) is a private deemed-to-be university in Sonipat, Haryana, near New Delhi, established in 2009 and recognized as an Institute of Eminence by the UGC in 2020. Offering 45 multidisciplinary programmes across law, liberal arts, life sciences, and business, JGU is committed to academic freedom, global collaboration, and developing future leaders who drive societal transformation.
