APIGA India Appointed Coordinator for Safer Internet Day India

APIGA India is honored to coordinate Safer Internet Day India, supporting safer and more responsible digital participation for young users.

Official SID India announcement shared by APIGA India.

What This Means

This role strengthens APIGA India's commitment to digital safety, youth awareness, and responsible Internet participation through public engagement and community action.

Focus Areas

  • Better Internet for Kids awareness
  • Safer online behavior and digital responsibility
  • Collaboration across youth, educators, and partners

About APIGA India's Role

As coordinator, APIGA India supports outreach, information flow, and partnership alignment for Safer Internet Day India activities.
